What works today

The online editor generates timed subtitles with a multilingual browser model, then lets you correct them, import SRT, choose colours and export. Model downloads and transcription need enough device memory and may take several minutes. The separate Windows edition handles longer videos and offers local clip suggestions. A mobile browser may offer fewer video export formats.
