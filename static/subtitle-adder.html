Add subtitles to videos in Tamil, Tanglish and English.

Upload your own video, generate a draft transcript, correct each caption, choose a colour and export a vertical video or SRT file. Processing runs on your computer.

Open the subtitle editor ↗

This editor currently runs on the computer where Cliploom is installed. A hosted multi-user edition is in development.

How it works

  1. Upload an MP4, MOV, WebM or MKV file up to 10 minutes.
  2. Choose Tamil, English or mixed Tamil-English speech. Add names and special terms to help recognition.
  3. Review and edit the generated subtitles.
  4. Pick from six caption colours and export a vertical MP4 or SRT file.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cliploom free?

The local edition is free and open source. Your hardware, internet and electricity still have costs.

Does it support Tanglish?

Yes. Choose Tamil + English for mixed speech. The transcription model may still make mistakes, so review the text before sharing.

Where are my videos stored?

The local edition stores original videos, drafts and exports in its data folder on your computer. It does not upload them to a Cliploom cloud account.

Can I change subtitle colours?

Yes. Choose white, yellow, mint, coral, lavender or sky blue before exporting.
